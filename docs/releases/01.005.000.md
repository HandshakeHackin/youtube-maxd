# YouTube Max’d 1.5.0

- **Your feed, your way:** mute title phrases and put locally followed channels first.
- **Playback rescue:** check playback health and try a lower quality or AVC format without losing your place.
- **Smoother phone remote:** better reconnect, timeouts, queue focus and protection against repeated taps.
- **Better bookmarks and menus:** cleaner names, fewer duplicates, clearer save errors and improved Back navigation.
- **Update notices:** background checks for new stable releases, with manual checks and a download QR in About.

App data is preserved. Picture-in-picture stays off by default.

**Install/update:** download **PPSA99991.zip**, close the app, extract it and replace the complete **PPSA99991** app folder in your ShadowMount+ location. Keep your separate app data folder. The source ZIP is for developers.

Intended for PS5 firmware 11.00 with ShadowMount+. Automated checks, PS5 cross-compilation and ZIP validation are recorded with the release; physical PS5 acceptance remains pending. This version adds update notifications, rather than automatic installation. Older builds need this update installed first.

Built on [ProsperoTube](https://github.com/LedyBacer/ProsperoTube) by BlackBearReloaded and contributors, and [EVO Player](https://github.com/sainsaji/EVO-PLAYER-PS5). Matching source, licenses and checksums are included.
