The first public release of ProsperoTube: a native YouTube client for PlayStation 5 homebrew. It runs on the console by itself — the YouTube service is built into the app, so no computer, companion or Google API key is involved.

This is an early release. Guest browsing and playback have passed sessions on PS5 consoles; accounts, phone casting and the new Afterglow interface are newer and still being validated. Please report problems through [GitHub issues](https://github.com/blackbearreloaded/ProsperoTube/issues).

## Highlights

- **Self-sufficient.** YouTube.js runs inside the app on QuickJS-NG; video streams straight from YouTube's servers to the PS5 hardware decoders.
- **Playback.** AVC, VP9 and HEVC up to 2160p in hardware, AV1 in software, HDR (PQ and HLG), automatic frame-rate switching and rewind in live streams.
- **Your choice per video.** Quality, audio track (dubs, original, described audio) and captions — including YouTube's auto-generated and auto-translated tracks, and two languages at once.
- **Guest or signed in.** Everything browses without an account. Choose **Sign in to YouTube** on the main menu to add your account with a code entered on your phone or computer: subscriptions, history, Watch later and playlists.
- **Two interfaces.** The Classic browser (default) and Afterglow (preview), a new console-first interface with four looks, its own sounds and 21 languages.
- **Integrations.** SponsorBlock with skip review and Undo, DeArrow titles and thumbnails, phone casting from the YouTube app (experimental) and subscriptions import from NewPipe, PocketTube or GrayJay.

## Install

1. Download `PPSA99142.zip` below and check it against `SHA256SUMS`.
2. Fully close ProsperoTube if it is running.
3. Extract the ZIP and copy the complete `PPSA99142` folder to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, so that `/data/homebrew/PPSA99142/eboot.bin` exists. Do not upload the ZIP itself.
4. Start ProsperoTube.

It needs a PS5 that runs homebrew, a title loader that registers apps from `/data/homebrew` (such as ShadowMountPlus), and Lapy for access to its data folder `/data/prosperotube`. See the [README](https://github.com/blackbearreloaded/ProsperoTube#getting-started) for details and controls.

The release ZIP is built by GitHub Actions from this tag. Verify it with:

```
gh attestation verify PPSA99142.zip -R blackbearreloaded/ProsperoTube
```

## Known limitations

- Videos that need the signed-in account (age-restricted, private, members-only) do not play yet; signed-in profiles play videos as Guest does.
- The player's own controls and menus are in English.
- Classic remains the default interface until every Afterglow screen has been validated on a console.
- Sign-ins are stored as plain text in `/data/prosperotube/accounts` (the PS5 has no encrypted storage).
- ProsperoTube talks to YouTube as one client identity: when YouTube changes its service, playback can stop until an update arrives.
