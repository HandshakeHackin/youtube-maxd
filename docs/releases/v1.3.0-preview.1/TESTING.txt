YouTube Max’d 1.3.0 / v1.3.0-preview.1 — console testing guide
Intended setup: PS5 firmware 11.00, ShadowMount+.
Title: PPSA99991. Content version: 01.003.000.

This exact integrated build has not been tested on a PS5. The host checks do
not establish hardware support. Keep a backup of app data before testing.

INSTALL
Extract PPSA99991.zip from the 1.3.0 preview release. Copy the complete
PPSA99991 folder to the location scanned by ShadowMount+, commonly
/data/homebrew/PPSA99991. Keep assets, the voice model, helper and manifest.
Use the console's existing ELF loader on TCP 9021 or compatible resident Lapy.
Avoid overwriting another app with the same development title ID.

CHECK ON THE CONSOLE
1. Launch, browse as Guest, search and cleanly exit. Confirm saved settings
   survive restart under /data/youtube-maxd. Try Classic and Afterglow.
2. Play public videos at 720p/1080p first. Seek, pause, resume and switch video
   formats and audio tracks with R2. Test higher resolutions/codecs separately;
   record the actual format, frame rate, HDR mode and duration tested.
3. Enable captions, chapters, SponsorBlock and DeArrow separately. Confirm
   originals/undo remain available where offered. Test a live/DVR stream.
4. Sign in through Google's TV activation page, view subscriptions/library,
   restart and confirm refresh. Review account writes before sending them.
   Removing a local account does not revoke its Google authorization.
5. Connect and unmute the DualSense microphone. Hold L3, wait for Listening,
   speak and release. Test Circle cancellation, silence, six-second auto-stop,
   repeated queries, mute/reconnect, profile change and exit during recognition.
   Record recognition time and memory/performance symptoms.
6. Create/edit/reopen/delete a Smart playlist. Test account and local sources,
   matching rules, restart persistence, unwatched markers, profile isolation,
   slow refreshes and next/previous inside the filtered playback snapshot.
7. Auto Max is off by default. Enable it, then test sustained buffering and
   decoding pressure. Confirm saved limits and manual selections win, live
   streams are excluded and audio/captions/position survive a format change.
   A short rebuffer is possible. Repeat with it disabled.
8. Check red/white DualSense effects in menus, loading, listening, playing,
   pause and errors; test the feedback switch, reconnect and exit cleanup.
   This app does not control console chassis LEDs.
9. Phone casting and background audio are experimental. Test their ownership,
   exit and interaction with voice/profile switching separately.

NEW VIEWING FEATURES
10. PiP must be off at startup. In Afterglow, select R2 -> Picture-in-picture;
    browse/search, tap L3 to return, then change video/profile and restart.
    Confirm it stays off until explicitly selected again. Test regular audio-
    only background browsing separately. Check SDR/HDR and each codec in PiP.
11. R2 -> Transcript & bookmarks: search available captions, seek to a result,
    save/delete named timestamps, restart and check per-profile isolation.
12. During playback hold L3: test pause, resume, forward 30 seconds, captions
    off, quality 1080p and add to queue. Test cancellation and unknown commands.
13. In YouTube preferences, build sessions for several 15-120-minute budgets.
    Confirm total selected video runtime fits and watched videos are excluded.
14. Test all four local family slots with a parent PIN, approved channel IDs,
    search restriction and short viewing limit. Check resolved-channel denial
    via search, queues, direct links and phone launch. Check PIN on exit,
    restart persistence, separate histories/bookmarks and background/PiP usage.
15. Start a party room, scan the QR on phones on the same LAN and test guest
    suggestions/votes, host approval/rejection, removal, expiration and profile
    change. Restart the app and confirm the room is off. No router forwarding.

REPORT
Include the version/build ID, firmware, launcher, interface, steps, actual
video ID/codec/resolution and sanitized logs. Do not post passwords, tokens,
cookies, private account files or authenticated stream URLs.
https://github.com/HandshakeHackin/youtube-maxd/issues

LIMITS
Private/age-restricted/members-only/paid playback is off by default; sign-in
does not guarantee it. 4K/HDR/AV1 throughput and microphone routing are unverified.
YouTube or community APIs may change. Automatic updates are disabled.
