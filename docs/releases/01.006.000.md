# YouTube Max’d 1.6.0

- Continue Watching and subscription catch-up queues.
- Better phone keyboard search, sleep timers, playback presets and accessibility options.
- Per-channel quality, speed, captions and volume leveling.
- Interrupted-session recovery with saved positions and queue review.
- Playback diagnostics and hold-Touchpad Quick Switch.
- Thumbnail RAM cleanup, temporary-caption cleanup and offline backup previews.

Install `PPSA99991.zip`: close the app, extract it and replace the complete app folder. Keep the separate app data folder. Picture-in-picture remains off by default.

Intended for firmware 11.00 with ShadowMount+. Automated tests and PS5 cross-compilation are separate from physical console testing, which remains pending. Channel leveling requires available loudness metadata; recovery does not autoplay. Google-linked profile import remains research.

Based on ProsperoTube and EVO Player. Thanks to their contributors; credits, licenses and corresponding source are included.
